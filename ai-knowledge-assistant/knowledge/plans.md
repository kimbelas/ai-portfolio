# Plans and Pricing

Acme Cloud Backup offers three plans.

The Free plan costs nothing and includes 5 GB of storage. It is limited to a single device and community support.

The Pro plan costs $9 per month, billed monthly, and includes 1 TB of storage, unlimited devices, file versioning, and priority email support. An annual option is $90 per year.

The Business plan costs $25 per user per month and adds centralized administration, single sign-on (SSO), and audit logs. Business plans require a minimum of three seats.

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
