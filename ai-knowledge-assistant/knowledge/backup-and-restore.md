# Backups and Restore

Once installed, Acme backs up your files automatically every hour. You can also trigger a manual backup at any time.

File versioning keeps previous versions of changed files. The Free plan keeps versions for 30 days; the Pro and Business plans keep versions for 1 year.

To restore a file, open the Acme app, go to the History tab, select a backup point, and click Restore. You can restore a single file or an entire folder.

Deleted files can be recovered for up to 30 days on the Free plan and up to 1 year on the Pro and Business plans.
