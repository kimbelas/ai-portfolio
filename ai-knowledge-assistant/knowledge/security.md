# Security and Compliance

Acme encrypts all data with AES-256, both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest.

Acme uses a zero-knowledge architecture: encryption keys are derived from your password and never leave your device, so Acme staff and employees cannot read the contents of your files.

Acme is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Data centers are located in the United States and the European Union; you choose your region at signup.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on all plans and is required for Business plan administrators.
