# Account and Billing

You can export all of your files as a ZIP archive at any time from the Account page.

If you cancel your subscription, your account switches to read-only mode. Your data remains accessible for 60 days, after which it is permanently deleted.

Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase under the money-back guarantee. Annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis after the first 30 days.

To permanently delete your account and all data immediately, use the Delete Account option in Settings. This action cannot be undone.
