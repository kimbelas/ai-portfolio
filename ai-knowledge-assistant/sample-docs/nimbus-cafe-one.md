The Nimbus Café One is a compact home espresso machine. It retails for $349 and comes in matte black or cream.

## Specs
- 15-bar Italian pump for espresso extraction.
- 1.5-liter removable water tank.
- Heats up in about 30 seconds (thermoblock).
- Dimensions: 24 cm wide, 30 cm tall.

## What it makes
It makes espresso, ristretto, and Americano. It does NOT include a built-in milk frother — a manual steam wand is sold separately as the Nimbus Steam Kit ($59). There is no smartphone app; all controls are physical buttons on the front panel.

## Care and support
Descale every 3 months with the Nimbus descaling solution. The machine carries a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Support is email-only, with a 48-hour response target.
